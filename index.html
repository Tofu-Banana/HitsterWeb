HitsterWebiPhone setup (one time only)

On iPhone, HitsterWeb controls your music app (Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music) through Apple's Shortcuts app. Make this shortcut once (5 actions):

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name the shortcut exactly HitsterWeb.
  2. Add Skip Forward.
  3. Add Seek. Tap its time and choose Shortcut Input. (If it won't take it, type 0:45 instead.)
  4. Add Play/Pause and tap it to change it to Play.
  5. Add Wait and set it to 30 seconds.
  6. Add Play/Pause and change it to Pause. Done!

To test: start any playlist in your music app, then tap Test. It should skip to the next song, jump to 0:30, play 30 seconds, pause, and come back here.

Load your playlist

  1. Use a playlist you own in Spotify (or copy the songs into a new playlist of yours).
  2. Tap Open Exportify, log in with Spotify, and tap Export next to your playlist. A .csv file downloads.
  3. Come back here and tap Upload playlist file.
  1. On a computer (Music on Mac, or iTunes on Windows): select the playlist, then File → Library → Export Playlist. Save it as XML or Text and send the file to this phone.
  2. Or on this phone: tap Open TuneMyMusic, choose Apple Music as the source, pick your playlist, and choose Export to file (CSV).
  3. Come back here and tap Upload playlist file.
  1. Tap Open TuneMyMusic and choose YouTube Music as the source.
  2. Pick your playlist, choose Export to file as the destination (CSV), and download the file.
  3. Come back here and tap Upload playlist file.
